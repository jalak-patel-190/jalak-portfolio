Case 04 · Reading the tracker

Reading the tracker

Like-for-like measurement across about fifty accounts so growth claims stayed tied to comparable windows, not vanity totals.

Context

Many accounts, one measurement problem

Without a shared tracker logic, each platform and language surface told a different story. Decisions needed comparable windows.

Problem

Totals hide the truth

Lifetime numbers mix tenure work with older catalogue performance. Like-for-like periods are what make a change defensible.

Strategy

Fix the window before the story

Standardise how accounts are compared so content and distribution choices can be judged against the same baseline.

Takeaway

Measurement is strategy

The tracker is not a report — it is the rule set that keeps regional and corporate claims honest.

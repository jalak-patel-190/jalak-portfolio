AJMERA FASHION

A B2B textile manufacturer's regional content portfolio, and what my first digital marketing job taught me about running one.

Duration
Jul 2022 to Jul 2023
Role
Digital Marketing Executive
Scope
YouTube, Facebook, Instagram
Accounts
About 50 across platforms

Company

WHO AJMERA FASHION IS

Ajmera Fashion is a textile manufacturer and wholesaler specialising in sarees and traditional Indian clothing for women and men.

Its real business is helping small retailers and first time entrepreneurs start their own clothing businesses, and inventory sales pay for that. YouTube was never the revenue engine. It was how the company earned trust with buyers in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities before they placed an order.

How the digital side was set up

A large set of accounts split by language, by product category and by business model, across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and a few other platforms. The team tracked about fifty of them every day, and I maintained that tracker.

My role

A FRESHER, INSIDE A TEAM

Where I started

Ajmera Fashion was my first job in digital marketing. I joined in July 2022 as Digital Marketing Executive with no prior experience.

How the scope grew

I started on planning and scheduling for the regional channels. Over the year I added scripting, supervising designers, community posts, content for the founder with the PR team, event content for HR, and the daily growth tracker. About six months in, I was trusted to interview candidates for the company's internship programme.

What I owned

  • Content planning and posting schedules for the regional channels
  • Scripting videos and planning festive content
  • Supervising designers so creative stayed aligned with the plan
  • Community posts, including festival posts and quizzes
  • Reading YouTube Analytics to decide which products each channel should lean into
  • The founder and brand ambassador content calendar, alongside the PR team
  • The Life at Ajmera pages and event content for HR
  • The daily growth tracker
  • Seven search phrases on the team's keyword roster
Official title: Digital Marketing Executive. I worked inside a team led by my team leader. The language map, the title sheet and the keyword roster belonged to the team, and each case says which pieces were mine.

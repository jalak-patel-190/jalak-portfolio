Context

DIGITAL ALONE WAS NOT THE FULL SURFACE

Wholesale buyers still meet brands in trade spaces and local retail. Offline assets had to match the digital story.

Problem

SPLIT CHANNELS WITHOUT SHARED MESSAGING

If offline and online say different things, trust breaks at the first real-world touch.

Strategy

ONE MESSAGE, TWO SURFACES

Scripts for product, wholesale opportunity, retailer education, and brand storytelling. Offline banners, pamphlets, trade presence, and local partnerships under the same budget logic.

Execution

WHAT WAS PRODUCED

Business-focused video scripts and production support, plus offline marketing placements coordinated with digital campaigns.

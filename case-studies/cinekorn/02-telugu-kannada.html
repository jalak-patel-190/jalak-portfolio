Case 02 · Telugu and Kannada

Telugu and Kannada channel systems

Regional language channels with their own catalogue logic so distribution matched language demand.

Context

Language is a distribution decision

Telugu and Kannada audiences needed dedicated surfaces. A single Hindi or pan-India feed could not carry regional title demand.

Strategy

Separate systems, shared discipline

Run language channels with consistent publishing rules while keeping title selection matched to each audience.

Evidence

Channel analytics

Takeaway

Regional channels need ownership

Language surfaces work when catalogue selection and cadence are intentional — not when they are leftovers from a central feed.

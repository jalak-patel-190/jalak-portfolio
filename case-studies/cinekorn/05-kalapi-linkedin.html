Case 05 · Kalapi LinkedIn

Founder Presence, Not Just a Company Page

Narrative, cadence, and positioning for Kalapi Nagada on LinkedIn, run alongside the corporate channel so the founder voice stays distinct.

Founder profile
Narrative system
Cadence owned

Context

A founder channel next to a company channel

Kalapi Nagada is a founder of Cinekorn Entertainment. The personal LinkedIn presence needed to reflect industry positioning without becoming a second company bulletin.

Problem

Company updates are not founder narrative

If the founder feed only repeats announcements, it adds little beyond the corporate page. The gap was a distinct point of view: how the business of entertainment is read from the inside.

Strategy

Separate roles, shared standards

Company page = institutional voice. Founder profile = perspective, pattern recognition, and industry context. Cadence and topic selection were managed so the two feeds reinforced rather than duplicated each other.

Evidence

Profile and performance surface

Content system (focus areas)

Relative emphasis across the founder feed

  • Industry context and pattern posts
  • Founder business point of view
  • Selective company-linked signal

Bars show relative emphasis in the system, not a public ranking of post performance.

Founder profile and analytics surfaces used for cadence and positioning review.

Results

A clearer founder lane

The founder feed carries industry and business framing instead of only company announcements. It sits next to the corporate page without replacing it. Live profile: Kalapi Nagada on LinkedIn.

Takeaway

What this showed

Founder LinkedIn works best when it is treated as a distinct narrative system, not as a mirror of the company page.

Case 05 · Corporate content

Corporate content system

A publishing system for corporate surfaces so brand messaging stayed consistent while regional accounts handled local demand.

Context

Corporate and regional need different jobs

Corporate feeds had to represent the organisation. Regional feeds had to sell. Mixing those jobs confused both audiences.

Problem

One cadence does not fit all

Without a clear corporate lane, posts either went quiet or duplicated regional product noise.

Strategy

Separate the lanes

Define what corporate publishes, how often, and how it supports regional demand without competing with it.

Takeaway

System over sporadic posts

Corporate content works when ownership and cadence are explicit — not when it is whatever is left after regional work.

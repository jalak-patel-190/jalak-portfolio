Case 01 · Cinekorn Entertainment

Turning a Manual Data Task Into a Content Decision

Content work does not always begin with a creative brief. Sometimes it begins with a messy spreadsheet.

0 rows classified
0 days to finish
AI + human review workflow

Context

Where the work started

Early in the Cinekorn role, I received multiple files containing roughly 6,000 to 8,000 rows of data that needed to be sorted and classified by tier before the information could support further analysis.

The task itself was not complicated. The problem was the volume of repetitive work.

Problem

Organise data without wasting judgment time

Two paths: do the entire task manually, or remove the repetitive part while keeping human review over the final output. The second option made more sense.

Strategy

The decision

I used Gemini to help generate the Python workflow and ran the process through Google Colab. AI built the repetitive workflow. Judgment stayed human.

Goal was not "use AI." Goal was remove unnecessary manual work while keeping control over the result.

Evidence

Workflow

Raw files→ AI-assisted Python→ Classification→ Manual check→ Usable dataset

Time cost: manual vs assisted

Illustrative comparison of repetitive classification effort

  • Full manual review of thousands of rows
  • Workflow + human check in about two days

AI handled repetitive classification. Final judgment and corrections stayed human.

Results

What changed

Approximately 6,000 to 8,000 rows processed and classified within two days. Time moved toward work that required judgment.

Takeaway

What this showed

AI was useful because it solved a specific bottleneck. The important skill is knowing where it can remove repetitive work, where it needs checking, and where the decision still belongs to the person doing the work.

Case 03 · Jalan Mega Mart

LEADING A TELE-CALLING TEAM

Four agents, a daily target and a bill for every conversation that converted. The team report records bills across the months it covers.

4 agents153 bills with readable amounts₹0.5 crore bills recorded in the report*

Context

SALES THAT NEEDED A PHONE

Some of the company’s buyers were reached by phone. Calling was the direct route from a marketing message to an order.

Problem

A TEAM WITHOUT A DAILY TARGET DRIFTS

Without a daily record, calls and messages go untracked and nobody can see which conversations turn into bills.

Strategy

DAILY ACTIVITY, TRACKED TO A BILL

Calls and messages
Video calls and visits
Share a catalogue PDF
Bill or sale order
Follow up pending bills

Execution

HOW I LED THE TEAM

  • Set and tracked daily activity for each agent.
  • Motivated the team to convert conversations into bills.
  • Recorded every conversation outcome, including pending bills.

Evidence

WHAT THE REPORT RECORDS

ActivityWhat the report shows
Calls and messagesDaily counts per agent across the report months
Video calls and visitsRecorded per day per agent
Catalogue sharesRecorded per day per agent
BillsBill numbers with amounts, tracked per day

*Readable bills total about ₹53 lakh across 153 unique bill numbers. The report is a partial export, so some cells are unreadable and the true figure is higher. The report covers August to November 2024 and March to May 2025, not a full year.

Results

WHAT THIS PROVES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

The report proves the team worked daily and tracked bills. It does not prove that the influencer campaigns caused those sales, and it is not the company’s full annual revenue.

What this showed

A DAILY RECORD TURNS EFFORT INTO A MANAGEABLE TEAM

When every conversation has a line in the record, a team of four can be managed day by day and its results can be defended.

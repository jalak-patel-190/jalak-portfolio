Case 08 · Swad

3–4 CUTDOWNS PER LONG VIDEO

Every long-form piece fed a short-form engine across Swad and Sneha channels, plus original vertical concepts.

3–4 shorts eachIG + YT verticalOriginal formats

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

Long-form alone under-uses each shoot day.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

Without a cutdown system, promotion depends on the algorithm finding the long video.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

For every long video, cut three to four shorts or reels. Direct most of that vertical output. Add original short-form concepts beyond pure promo.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Standing short-form engine plus original series R&D.

Evidence

SIGNAL

Funnel shape

Reach

Funnel from long asset to vertical distribution.

Takeaway

WHAT THIS SHOWED

Short-form is a distribution system, not a separate creative hobby.

Case 04 · Jalan Mega Mart

Festival demand and content planning

Aligning product push, channel content, and retailer timing around festival demand peaks — not a decorative calendar, but an operations rhythm.

Context

Demand clusters around festivals

For a B2B textile wholesaler, festival periods concentrate buyer attention and stock decisions. Content and outreach had to match those windows rather than run on a flat weekly cadence.

Problem

Flat posting misses the season

Without a clear pre-festival, peak, and post-festival plan, campaigns and product messaging arrived too early, too late, or without enough channel support.

Strategy

Plan the season, not just the day

Map major festival windows, decide which products and formats lead each phase, and sequence channel work so retailers and callers are not surprised by spikes.

Execution

What I coordinated

  • Festival windows tied to product and content priorities.
  • Channel posts and promotions timed to demand, not habit.
  • Alignment with tele-calling and retailer-facing updates where needed.

What this showed

Calendar as operations, not decoration

Festival planning worked when it was treated as demand logistics: what to push, when to publish, and who follows up.

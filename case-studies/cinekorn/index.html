Cinekorn Entertainment

Building and operating a multi-channel content distribution system across YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

DurationJanuary 2026 to Present
RoleSenior Social Media Manager
ScopeYouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn
Accounts9 platforms

Who they are

Cinekorn Entertainment is a Mumbai-based film distribution, production, and content syndication company founded in 2010.

What they do

The company handles theatrical releases, satellite distribution, and OTT syndication across Bollywood, South Indian, regional Gujarati, Marathi, and Hollywood titles.

How the business works

Licensed film IP is extended into digital long-form, serialised, and short-form content across multiple platforms.

Where I started

I joined as Senior Social Media Manager responsible for digital movie content strategy, platform revenue, and LinkedIn presence for both the company and the founder.

How scope evolved

The role expanded into full portfolio management across six YouTube properties, Facebook recovery, and two LinkedIn pages.

What I manage today

Nine live accounts across YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Responsibilities include cadence planning, catalog health, format extraction, cross-platform funnels, and transparent reporting.

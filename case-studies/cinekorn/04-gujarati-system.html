Case 04 · Gujarati system

Gujarati catalogue system

Building and running a Gujarati distribution surface with clear tenure vs catalogue performance boundaries.

Context

Gujarati needed its own lane

Regional demand for Gujarati titles required a dedicated publishing system, not occasional posts on a general channel.

Strategy

System before volume

Set cadence, title selection rules, and measurement so growth could be attributed to the work period, not only lifetime catalogue.

Evidence

Analytics

Takeaway

Language systems compound

A stable Gujarati system creates a surface that can absorb catalogue and new titles without restarting from zero each month.

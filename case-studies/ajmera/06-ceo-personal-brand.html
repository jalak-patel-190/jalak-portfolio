Case 06 · Ajmera Fashion

FROM CEO ACTIVITIES TO PERSONAL BRAND CONTENT

Building a personal content calendar around daily activities, employee interactions and business videos for the CEO and Brand Ambassador.

5 public YouTube examples across three content directions

Context

THE CEO NEEDED A CONTENT SYSTEM, NOT ONE-OFF POSTS

While working at Ajmera Fashion, responsibilities expanded beyond the regional social channels. After learning the wider content operation, the work included collaboration with the CEO’s PR team.

The subject was the person behind the business: the CEO and Brand Ambassador, his activities, his interactions with employees, and his business perspective. Daily activities and business moments were available, but without a calendar they stayed irregular.

Problem

ACTIVITY WITHOUT A CALENDAR

A personal brand cannot run only when something important happens. A CEO has a constant stream of material: meetings, people, decisions, business activity and everyday moments. Without planning, that material remains scattered and content becomes occasional. Buyers and partners needed repeated, clear contact with the CEO and brand ambassador roles.

Strategy

PERSONAL CONTENT CALENDAR

Build a calendar around daily activities, employee interactions and business videos. Treat the CEO and Brand Ambassador tracks as ongoing systems, not occasional appearances. Turn everyday access into repeatable content opportunities without making every post feel like a corporate announcement.

Takeaway

THE CEO BECAME A SOURCE OF CONTENT, NOT JUST A NAME

A personal brand does not need a completely new idea every day if the person’s real work already contains useful material. Daily activity created recurring opportunities; employee interactions added a human dimension; business videos gave the CEO a direct communication role. A calendar made these easier to plan instead of treating them as one-off posts.

Case 01 · Swad

STUDENTS AS MARKETING ASSETS

The one-year diploma was the highest-ticket course. Most enrollments traced to Instagram and YouTube, so live sessions with current students became the funnel.

Diploma flagshipIG + YT sourceStudents on camera

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

The One-Year Diploma in Culinary Arts and Bakery combined months of hands-on training with internship. Enrollments clustered on Instagram and YouTube.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

Promotional content that only talked about the course did not put proof of skill in public.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

Partner chefs for live sessions featuring current students, so learners became visible proof and shared their own appearances.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Live sessions designed so students could showcase skill, build confidence, and organically extend reach.

Evidence

SIGNAL

Funnel shape

Public student appearances as enrollment proof.

Takeaway

WHAT THIS SHOWED

Confidence on camera is a distribution channel when students share the session themselves.

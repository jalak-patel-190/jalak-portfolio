Company

WHO SWAD IS

Swad Cooking Institute sells cooking and baking courses. The offer runs from offline masterclasses and a one year diploma to online recipe programmes, free workshops and a membership.

After two years in B2B textile marketing, joining a B2C education business meant a different kind of audience. Those viewers were learners, not buyers of stock, and the content had to earn trust before a course fee was even considered.

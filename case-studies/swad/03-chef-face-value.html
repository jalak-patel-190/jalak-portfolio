Case 03 · Swad

MATCH TOPIC TO TALENT

Chefs carry different on-camera strength by topic. Content was matched to that face-value instead of one house style for everyone.

Chef roster mappedTopic-fit ruleEveryday wins

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

Embedded time with the chef team showed that face-value is topic-specific.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

Forcing every chef into the same topic set wastes trust.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

Map each chef to recipes and subjects that fit their on-camera strength. Prefer everyday topics with one new fact over pure novelty.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Roster-level topic matching across chefs, including the owner.

Evidence

SIGNAL

Focus rings

Where attention was spent

HighCore
MedSupport

Pattern: familiar topics with one new method beat novel recipes.

Takeaway

WHAT THIS SHOWED

Talent has a topic-fit, not only a follower count.

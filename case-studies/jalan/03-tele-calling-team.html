Case 03 · Jalan Mega Mart

Tele-calling team and performance systems

Coordination between digital demand and the tele-calling team so enquiries did not stall between platforms and the phone floor.

Context

Digital work only matters if the call team can act on it

Leads and interest generated online still had to reach a person who could close or qualify.

Strategy

Make the handoff part of the plan

Define how enquiries move from digital surfaces into the calling queue, what information travels with them, and how performance is reviewed together.

What this showed

Marketing is not finished at the click

For a B2B textile wholesaler, the system only works when digital demand and human follow-up are designed as one path.

Case 04 · Jalan

TWENTY-PLUS CREATORS, ONE BUDGET FRAME

Research, outreach, negotiation, and delivery tracking for more than twenty influencers under a roughly two-lakh annual allocation.

20+ influencers₹2L budgetOnline + offline mix

Context

NO INFLUENCER BENCH

Jalan had no existing creator relationships. The budget had to cover both discovery and delivery.

Problem

OUTREACH WITHOUT A SYSTEM WASTES BUDGET

Picking creators without briefs, rates, and delivery checks burns the allocation without learning.

Strategy

BENCH, BRIEF, TRACK

Identify creators across textile, fashion, and business niches. Negotiate rates. Brief delivery. Track what actually posted.

Execution

WHAT HAPPENED

More than twenty influencers negotiated and directed under a roughly ₹2 lakh frame that mixed online and offline placements.

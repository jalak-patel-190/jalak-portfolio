Context: Sister Company of Shalini Fashion

Jalan Mega Mart is a B2B textile company founded by the same group behind Shalini Fashion. Shalini Fashion has dominated the Banaras (Varanasi) textile market for years. Jalan Mega Mart launched as a strategic pivot: a Surat-based manufacturing-to-wholesaler operation.

The Business Model: By 2023, every textile company was trying to go B2B (wholesale direct to retailers). Shalini Fashion decided to launch Jalan as a separate B2B entity. Own manufacturing quality + wholesale reach = competitive advantage in an increasingly crowded B2B textile market.

When I joined, the company was 3-4 months old. They needed someone to build marketing from scratch.

The Challenge: Building Marketing at Zero

Official Title: Social Media Manager

Actual Scope (discovered after 4-5 months): I was managing the entire marketing team.

This wasn't a typical "social media manager" role. The company had:

  • No content strategy
  • No marketing team structure
  • No existing YouTube presence
  • No social media accounts
  • No influencer relationships
  • No budget allocation framework

And I had to build all of it. Alone. Then manage it.

The Role Expanded Quickly: What started as "manage social media" became "manage the entire marketing function, all teams, all budgets, all strategies."

The Role Expansion (The Real Story)

Job Title: Social Media Manager

Actual Role: Head of Marketing Operations

This wasn't a miscommunication. This was entrepreneurial necessity.

As a 3-4 month old company trying to scale a B2B textile operation, they couldn't afford to hire separate people for: - Social media - Influencer marketing - Creative management - Team leadership - Budget allocation - Offline marketing

So one person (me) ended up doing all of it. And making it work.

What This Proved: I could take a "social media manager" job description and scale it into a complete marketing function. Not because the job was written that way, but because I saw what was needed and built it.

Key Learnings

1. Job Titles Are Flexible (You Define Your Role)

Started as "Social Media Manager." Became de facto "Head of Marketing Operations" because the business needed it. Don't wait for job descriptions to expand. See what's needed and build it.

2. Influencer Marketing Is Negotiation + Direction

Finding influencers is easy. Negotiating rates is medium. But directing their content—telling them specifically which products, how to position them, what story to tell—that's what converts followers into customers.

3. Teams Need Leadership, Not Just Management

Managing a tele-calling team isn't about tracking calls. It's about motivation, coaching, showing them their work matters. A team that understands strategy performs better than a team that just executes tasks.

4. Scale Happens Through Systems & Delegation

I didn't personally make every video or design every graphic. I built a system where designers, videographers, and editors knew the strategy, the quality standards, and could execute independently.

5. Budget Allocation Drives Strategy

With ₹2 lakh, I had to choose: influencer marketing or paid ads or production quality. I split it strategically. Budget constraints force you to be intentional. That's good.

6. B2B Marketing Is Different

B2C wants engagement and virality. B2B wants trust and leads. Educational content, product knowledge, industry credibility matter more than follower counts.

Career Progression Moment

This role was pivotal. It wasn't just scaling what existed (Ajmera). It was building from scratch.

Ajmera: I learned systems thinking. Scaled existing operations. Got promoted to an internship role.

Jalan: I built a function. Managed teams. Allocated budgets. Made strategic decisions. Took initiative beyond my job description and made it work.

This is the transition from individual contributor to leader.

Building Marketing Functions

If you're starting a company or launching a new product line, you don't need to hire a full marketing team immediately. You need someone who can see what's needed and build it. Someone who can manage teams, allocate budgets, and execute across multiple channels.

That's the skill this role taught me.

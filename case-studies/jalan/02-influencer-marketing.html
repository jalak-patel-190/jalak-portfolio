Case 02 · Jalan Mega Mart

INFLUENCER MARKETING ON A MONTHLY BUDGET

Every booking is recorded. The tracker holds 102 bookings with 70 different influencers, and 15 influencers were booked more than once.

102 bookings tracked70 influencers₹2 lakh monthly budget

Context

A SMALL BUDGET, A LONG LIST OF NAMES

The company gave a monthly budget of ₹2 lakh for marketing, split between online and offline spend. Influencer marketing was the main online line.

Problem

INFLUENCERS ARE EASY TO FIND, HARD TO DIRECT

Finding an influencer takes a search. Getting content that sells a textile product takes negotiation, a brief on which product to feature, and someone present on the shoot day.

Strategy

FIND, NEGOTIATE, DIRECT, TRACK

Find the influencer
Negotiate the deal
Brief the product
Attend the shoot
Track the booking

Execution

HOW THE WORK RAN

  • Searched for influencers across the web and collected their contact details.
  • Negotiated rates and deliverables directly.
  • Guided each influencer on which product to feature.
  • Attended shoots when influencers came in.

Results

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS

The tracker proves the bookings and the spend pattern. It does not yet hold the reach, enquiry or sales result for each influencer, so this case makes no claim about return on that spend.

What this showed

A BOOKING LOG IS THE START OF ACCOUNTABILITY

Keeping one line per booking turned a list of influencers into a record of who came back. That record is what lets a small budget be defended.

Company

WHO JALAN MEGA MART IS

Jalan Mega Mart LLP is a sister company of Shalini Fashion, the textile business based in Banaras (Varanasi). It was set up in Surat as a manufacturer to wholesaler operation, at a time when most textile companies were moving to B2B. The advantage it started with was owning its own manufacturing quality.

I joined in July 2023, when the company was only three to four months old. The marketing function barely existed.

My role

A TITLE THAT DID NOT MATCH THE JOB

My official title was Social Media Manager, with operations oversight. Within four to five months I was running the whole marketing team, because the company had a small team and one job to be done.

Scope, in plain terms. I built the channel and page network, planned content and festival campaigns, ran influencer marketing, managed the tele-calling team and the creative team, and handled offline marketing, all inside one budget.

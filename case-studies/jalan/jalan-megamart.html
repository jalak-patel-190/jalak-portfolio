Primary case · Jalan Mega Mart

MARKETING OPERATIONS FROM ZERO

A new B2B textile wholesale brand with no content system, no channels, and no influencer relationships. This case covers what was built in the first year.

10-15 YouTube channels 5+ social pages ₹2L influencer budget 20+ influencers

Context

A NEW B2B BRAND FROM AN ESTABLISHED GROUP

Jalan Mega Mart is a B2B textile company from the group behind Shalini Fashion, a long-established Banaras (Varanasi) textile business. Jalan was the Surat-based pivot: own manufacturing quality sold wholesale to retailers in a market where every competitor was racing into B2B.

When I joined in July 2023, the company was about three to four months old. They needed marketing built from scratch, not optimised.

Problem

NO SYSTEM TO RUN

The official title was Social Media Manager. The starting point was closer to zero:

  • No content strategy
  • No marketing team structure
  • No YouTube presence
  • No social media accounts
  • No influencer relationships
  • No budget allocation framework

Within four to five months the scope had expanded to running the marketing function, not only posting.

Strategy

BUILD THE STACK, THEN RUN IT

The work had to create the operating system first, then keep it moving.

Channels
Creative calendar
Influencer bench

Execution

WHAT WAS BUILT IN YEAR ONE

YouTube portfolio

A set of channels aimed at different product and regional segments of the B2B textile audience, rather than one generic brand channel.

▶ Main Jalan channel

Marathi Shorts channel · Gujarati Shorts channel

Additional product-specific and regional channels made up the rest of the 10 to 15 channel portfolio. Detailed per-channel metrics will be added when the polished export is ready.

Social pages

Five or more Instagram and Facebook pages across product categories and regional markets, with a content calendar and daily posting rhythm.

Festival and seasonal campaigns

Calendars aligned to Diwali, Holi, regional festivals, B2B peak buying windows, and seasonal textile demand.

Influencer system

Negotiated and directed more than 20 influencers against a roughly ₹2 lakh annual allocation. The work was selection, brief, negotiation, and delivery tracking, not only outreach.

Team leadership

As the function grew, the role included directing writers, designers, and production support so output stayed on plan without every asset needing my hands on it.

Scripting, production, and offline

Scripts and video production support for the channel portfolio, plus offline marketing coordination where retail and wholesale events needed a digital counterpart.

Results

WHAT CAN BE STATED NOW

The primary, defensible outcomes from this tenure are the systems and assets that did not exist when I joined:

  • 10 to 15 YouTube channels created and operated
  • 5 or more social pages built and run
  • Influencer bench of 20+ creators under a ₹2L budget frame
  • A working content and campaign calendar tied to retail and wholesale cycles
What this case does not claim yet
  • It does not publish a full revenue attribution figure until the polished data export is ready.
  • It does not treat every channel as equal in performance. Per-channel numbers will be listed when verified.
  • Official title remained Social Media Manager. The operations scope is described as work done, not as a formal promotion.

What this showed

ZERO IS A SYSTEMS PROBLEM

Building marketing for a new B2B brand is not the same as growing an existing feed. The first job is to create channels, calendars, relationships, and a way for other people to produce inside the plan. Scale only starts after that stack exists.

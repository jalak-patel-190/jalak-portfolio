Case 04 · Gujarati distribution

One film, multiple content opportunities

Turn Gujarati film titles into full releases, serialised parts, Shorts, and Facebook distribution, with each format serving a different viewing behaviour.

17.77M tracked tenure views 163 pieces from one film 6.7× Facebook post volume

Context

A film is more than one upload

Gujarati film rights and existing titles create opportunities beyond a single full-length upload. Different formats can give the same film more ways to be discovered and watched across YouTube and Facebook.

Problem

One release limits the ways people can discover a film

A single upload limits the number of opportunities to reach viewers. Repurposing also needs clear measurement: legacy uploads should not be mixed into the results of work published during my tenure.

Decision

Match the format to the viewing behaviour

Use the full film for long-form viewing and search, serialised parts to create repeat viewing opportunities, Shorts for discovery, and Facebook cuts to extend distribution. Keep the reporting window clear so the results reflect the tenure work being assessed.

Result

One title became a wider distribution run

The Rakhewal run combined a fresh full release, serialised parts, and Shorts into one measured tenure total. Radha Tara's serialised re-release also gave an existing title a new distribution window. Facebook publishing volume increased after the restriction period; the observed change is reported without claiming the restriction was the only cause.

Takeaway

Distribution is a format decision, not just a publishing decision

A film can serve different viewing needs when the release plan accounts for long-form viewing, repeat visits, short-form discovery, and cross-platform distribution. The important part is to track each format clearly and separate tenure work from older catalogue activity.

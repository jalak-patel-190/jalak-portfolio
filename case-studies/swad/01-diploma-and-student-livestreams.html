Cases 01 and 02 · Swad Cooking Institute

STUDENTS AS MARKETING ASSETS

The one year diploma was the institute’s highest-ticket course. Its students became the best promoters it had, and a funny basic-course reel brought in leads.

1 year diploma with internship2nd best-selling course, basic cookingLive student sessions

Case 01 · Context

THE MOST VALUABLE COURSE NEEDED MORE STUDENTS

The one year culinary and bakery diploma combines six months of hands-on training with a six month internship. It is the biggest revenue course and the strongest credibility signal with five star hotels and competitors.

Most diploma enrolments came from Instagram and YouTube, so the marketing job was to grow that funnel.

Case 01 · Strategy

SHOW THE STUDENTS, AND LET THEM SHARE IT

Students get a public stage
Confidence builds
Students share their own appearance
Prospects see the course working
Enrolment and free PR

Case 01 · Execution

HOW THE STUDENT LIVESTREAMS WORKED

I worked with chefs to run live sessions that featured current students. The logic was simple. Students who are seen by the audience grow in confidence, and confident students share their appearance, which brings new viewers who can see exactly how the course works.

The result was higher views, stronger engagement, new viewers, more qualified leads for the sales team, and free PR for both chefs and students.

Case 02 · Context

THE SECOND BEST SELLER NEEDED A REASON TO BE SHARED

Basic Cooking was the second best selling course. Straight promotion was not going to travel, so the content had to be funny enough to share.

Case 02 · Execution

A FUNNY REEL THAT PEOPLE PASSED ON

I made a funny reel built around the basic course. It was designed to be shared, and it generated a strong volume of leads from those shares.

Basic cooking reel

Results

WHAT THE CASE SHOWS

Students became a funnel. Shares became leads. The institute’s biggest course gained visibility it could not buy directly.

What this showed

THE PEOPLE INSIDE THE COURSE ARE THE CONTENT

A course that a viewer can picture themselves in is easier to sell. Showing real students doing the work was more persuasive than any promotional line.

Case 03 · Jalan Mega Mart

Leading a tele-calling team

Coordinating tele-calling activity around daily targets, buyer follow-up, catalogue sharing and bill tracking.

Daily activity tracking Buyer follow-up Bill recording

Context

Sales that needed a phone

Some of the company's buyers were reached by phone. Calling was the direct route from a marketing message to an order.

Problem

A team without a daily target drifts

Without a daily record, calls and messages go untracked and nobody can see which conversations turn into bills.

Strategy

Daily activity, tracked to a bill

Calls and messages
Video calls and visits
Share a catalogue PDF
Bill or sale order
Follow up pending bills

Execution

How I led the team

  • Set and tracked daily activity for each agent.
  • Motivated the team to convert conversations into bills.
  • Recorded every conversation outcome, including pending bills.

Evidence boundary

Show the workflow, not an unverified total

The available portfolio archive supports tele-calling coordination and lead-generation work, but it does not include the underlying report needed to verify team size, bill totals or productivity changes. Those numbers are not presented as confirmed results.

To make this a quantified case, the source report would need to be checked for readable rows, date coverage, duplicate bill numbers and a clear definition of what each amount represents.

Results

Keep sales attribution separate

Tele-calling can connect marketing activity with buyer conversations, but this case does not claim that influencer campaigns caused any sales. No verified bill total or campaign-attributed revenue is published here.

What this showed

Consistent follow-up needs a clear record

Daily activity tracking, catalogue sharing and follow-up create a more manageable process. A reliable report is needed before claiming team productivity or sales outcomes.

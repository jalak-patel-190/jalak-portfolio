Case 02 · Regional channels

Telugu and Kannada channel systems

Building and operating regional language channels with their own catalog logic and growth patterns.

Context

Language markets need their own systems

Telugu and Kannada audiences were not a simple copy of the main catalogue strategy. Each channel needed titles, packaging, and cadence that matched how those markets actually watch.

Problem

One playbook does not fit every language

Treating regional channels as smaller versions of the primary feed underused both the catalogue and the audience.

Strategy

Separate systems, shared discipline

Run each language channel with its own selection logic and publishing rhythm, while keeping measurement consistent enough to compare.

Evidence

Channel analytics

Takeaway

Regional is a system choice

Growth in language channels came from treating them as distinct products, not as leftover inventory.

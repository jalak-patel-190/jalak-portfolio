Case 02 · Influencer marketing

Influencer marketing on a monthly budget

Finding, negotiating with and directing influencers, with bookings tracked one by one.

Context

A small budget, a long list of names

Influencer work at Jalan ran on a monthly budget. The job was to find people who fit the product, agree terms, brief them, and keep a record of every booking so spend could be defended.

What the tracker holds

One line per booking

Each row is a dated booking: influencer, date, amount, and status. Confirmed dated bookings total ₹15.2 lakh across 17 months. Twelve rows totalling ₹4.2 lakh are excluded here because they appear twice in the sheet and are being confirmed.

Visual summary of the booking log. The tracker proves bookings and spend pattern. Reach, enquiry, and sales per influencer are not claimed here.

Results

What the record shows

The tracker proves the bookings and the spend pattern. It does not yet hold the reach, enquiry or sales result for each influencer, so this case makes no claim about return on that spend.

What this showed

A booking log is the start of accountability

Keeping one line per booking turned a list of influencers into a record of who came back. That record is what lets a small budget be defended.

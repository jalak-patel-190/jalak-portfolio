CHANNELS BECAME PEOPLE

Within four to five months the work expanded from channels to tele-calling, creative, and production teams.

TITLE STAYED THE SAME

Problem

OUTPUT NEEDS MORE THAN ONE PAIR OF HANDS

Motivate and track tele-calling for leads. Direct creative and production so output stays aligned to the plan.

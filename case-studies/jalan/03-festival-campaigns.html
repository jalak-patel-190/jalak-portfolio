CALENDARS TIED TO BUYING CYCLES

Festival and seasonal campaigns aligned to Diwali, Holi, regional festivals, and B2B peak buying windows.

Festival offersB2B cyclesSeasonal textile

Context

TEXTILE DEMAND IS SEASONAL

Wholesale interest moves with festivals and climate. Content that ignores that calendar is always late.

Problem

GENERIC OFFERS MISS THE WINDOW

Without a planned festival and season map, creative arrives after the buying window has moved.

Strategy

MAP THE YEAR

Content and offer campaigns planned against festival seasons, B2B peak cycles, and seasonal textile demand such as summer silks and winter wear.

Execution

WHAT SHIPPED

Festival offer campaigns designed to drive wholesale inquiries, not only brand awareness.

Takeaway

THE CALENDAR IS THE STRATEGY

In textile B2B, timing is product strategy, not a design afterthought.

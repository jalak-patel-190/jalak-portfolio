Student live systems · Swad Cooking Institute

Students as marketing assets

The one-year diploma was the institute’s highest-ticket course. Students became its strongest promoters through live sessions and shareable proof.

1 year diploma with internship Live student sessions IG + YT enrolment funnel

Context

The most valuable course needed more students

The one-year culinary and bakery diploma combined six months of hands-on training with a six-month internship. It was the institute's highest-ticket course and its strongest credibility signal with five-star hotels and competitors.

Most diploma enrolments came from Instagram and YouTube, so the marketing job was to grow that funnel with proof that felt real.

Strategy

Show the students, and let them share it

Students get a public stage
Confidence builds
Students share their appearance
Prospects see the course working
Enrolment and free PR

Execution

Live student sessions

Students appeared on livestreams and shared those appearances. The content worked as proof of the course and as student-driven distribution. The basic cooking course also needed funny, shareable reels to pull leads without a large ad budget.

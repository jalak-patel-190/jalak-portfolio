Case 04 · Jalan Mega Mart

Festival calendar and offline marketing

A dated calendar of festivals and national days, built into posts and offers for every platform. Offline work ran alongside it on banners and pamphlets.

43 dates planned across seven months 3 platforms Offline banners and pamphlets

Context

A retailer calendar is a buying calendar

Retailers and buyers plan their stock around festivals. A post that lands before a festival is worth more than one that lands after it.

Problem

One post per day is not a plan

Without a dated list, posts go out whenever there is space. The calendar has to be built from the festival dates and the offers have to be tied to them.

Strategy

How festivals were chosen

Jalan sold textiles to buyers across India. The calendar was not limited to one state's festivals. It covered dates that mattered for stock and buying behaviour in different regions.

Examples of that logic:

  • Regional and state days (Ugadi, Gudi Padwa, Maharashtra Day, Gujarat Day, Telangana Formation Day, Onam) so regional channels had a reason to post on dates their audience already cared about.
  • Pan-India festivals (Raksha Bandhan, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Navratri, Dussehra, Dhanteras) tied to textile demand and gift buying.
  • National and observance days (Independence Day, Handloom Day, Heritage Day) used when a product or saree story fit the theme, not forced for every date.

Execution

What I planned and ran

  • Built the month by month content calendar and festival offer campaigns.
  • Planned posts for each platform, matched to the date.
  • Ran offline marketing alongside the digital plan: banner design and placement, and pamphlet production.

The calendar organized festival and campaign activity across the season. Results per campaign are not claimed here until offer and enquiry records are supplied.

Results

What the calendar covers

The calendar is the plan, not the performance report. It shows that every post had a date reason and that offline and digital work pulled in the same direction. Festival post examples can be added when YouTube links are provided.

What this showed

The calendar decides when the work is seen

Tying every post to a real date gave the plan a reason to exist on each day. For a multi-region textile network, that meant covering festivals across India, not only local ones.

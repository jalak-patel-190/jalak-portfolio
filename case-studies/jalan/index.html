WHO JALAN MEGA MART IS

Jalan Mega Mart is a B2B textile wholesale operation from the group behind Shalini Fashion. It launched as a Surat-based manufacturing-to-wholesaler pivot into a crowded B2B market.

How the digital side started

When I joined, the company was a few months old. Marketing had to be built from the ground up: channels, creative rhythm, influencer coordination, and regional performance frameworks. There was no existing stack to optimise.

My role

SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER

Where I started

I joined as Social Media Manager in July 2023. The official title stayed the same. The scope expanded into building and running marketing operations for a new brand.

What the scope covered

  • YouTube portfolio build-out across product and regional segments
  • Instagram and Facebook pages for product and regional markets
  • Festival and seasonal campaign calendars aligned to B2B buying cycles
  • Influencer coordination and creative operations

Case 02 · Ajmera Fashion

647 TO 14,473 FOLLOWERS, ORGANICALLY

One regional Facebook page, grown through Facebook groups with no paid promotion.

22x followers6.5 monthsOrganic growth

Context

ONE OF EIGHT FACEBOOK ACCOUNTS

The Gujarati Facebook page was one of eight Facebook accounts in the tracker I maintained. On 15 December 2022 it had 647 followers.

Across those eight accounts, Facebook followers grew from 53,109 to 75,962 by 1 July 2023, up 43.0%. That made Facebook the fastest growing platform in the portfolio.

Problem

A SMALL PAGE HAS LITTLE REACH OF ITS OWN

A page with a few hundred followers has little reach of its own. The audience had to be found where it already gathered.

Strategy

GO WHERE THE AUDIENCE ALREADY IS

The plan was to use Facebook groups as the distribution channel and to keep the growth organic, with no paid promotion.

Execution

WHAT I DID

Followers came through Facebook groups, organically. The tracker records the result rather than the posting log, so this case does not list group names or posting frequency.

Results

22 TIMES THE FOLLOWERS IN SIX AND A HALF MONTHS

The page went from 647 to 14,473 followers, a gain of 13,826. Two steps account for most of it: 3,137 followers between 1 February and 1 March, and 6,413 between 1 May and 1 June.

On the eight Facebook accounts compared, the combined gain was 22,853 followers. The Gujarati page produced 13,826 of them, about 60%.

What this showed

A FEED IS NOT THE ONLY WAY TO BE FOUND

A platform’s own feed is not the only distribution channel. Groups gave a small page an audience it could not have reached alone. The honest limit is that the tracker cannot say which groups or which posts caused the two steps.

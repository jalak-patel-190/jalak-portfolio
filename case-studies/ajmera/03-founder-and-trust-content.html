Case 03 · Ajmera Fashion

TRUST, FROM REAL PEOPLE

Educational videos, a brand ambassador and a founder series, built to answer one question: can I trust this seller?

9 public videos across three content tracks

Context

TRUST WAS THE BARRIER

Buyers needed more than product shots. They needed real people and clear answers before they would treat Ajmera as a reliable source for textile purchasing decisions.

Problem

PRODUCT SHOTS ALONE DID NOT CLOSE TRUST

Catalog imagery showed inventory. It did not answer who stands behind the business, how quality is handled, or why a buyer should switch from an existing supplier relationship.

Strategy

THREE CONTENT TRACKS AROUND ONE QUESTION

Every piece was built to answer: can I trust this seller? Three tracks carried that answer in different ways.

  • Educational videos — explain products, processes, and buying logic in plain language.
  • Brand ambassador content — a human face repeating consistency and care.
  • Founder series — ownership, values, and direct accountability on camera.

Execution

PUBLIC VIDEO PROOF ACROSS THE TRACKS

Nine public videos were produced and published across the three tracks so trust was demonstrated repeatedly, not claimed once in a single post.

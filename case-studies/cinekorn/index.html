Company

Who they are

Cinekorn Entertainment is a Mumbai-based film distribution, production, and content syndication company, incorporated in January 2010 under founders Kalapi Vinit Nagada and Ilachi Vinit Nagada.

What they do

The business runs theatrical releases, satellite distribution, and OTT syndication across Bollywood, South Indian, regional Gujarati and Marathi, and Hollywood titles, alongside a large digital footprint anchored by the Cinekorn Movies YouTube channel and regional sister channels.

How the business works

Licensed and owned film IP is the raw material. Digital channels extend that IP into long-form, serialised cuts, Shorts, and social clips. My work sits on that digital layer: turning one asset into multiple platform-native products without treating every channel as a copy of the same upload.

My Role

Where I started

I joined as Senior Social Media Manager with responsibility for digital movie content strategy, platform revenue, and LinkedIn for both the company and the founder.

How scope evolved

The seat expanded beyond single-channel posting into a portfolio problem: six YouTube properties, Facebook recovery after a long restriction, and two LinkedIn presences (company and founder). The job became designing one distribution logic that holds across all of them.

What I manage today

Nine live accounts across YouTube, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Cadence, catalog health, format extraction (full film to parts to Shorts), cross-platform funnels, and honest reporting of what is tenure-attributable versus inherited catalog.

Positioning note: Official title is Sr. Social Media Manager. The work product is closer to content systems and multi-account strategy. The case studies below show the scope without inflating the job title.

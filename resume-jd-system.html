Resume & JD System

I built this system so any hiring manager can get a clear and honest answer without guessing. Share a Job Description and receive a direct assessment based on my real work.

Step 1

Honest Assessment

You share the Job Description. I analyse it against my actual experience and give a clear answer: would I call this person for an interview, strengths vs gaps, and overall fit.

Step 2

Tailored Resume

If the fit is useful, I prepare a resume written specifically for that Job Description using real portfolio work. No invented numbers. No exaggerated claims.

What you need to share

  • 1 Company name
  • 2 Job title
  • 3 Full Job Description
  • 4 Short company brief (optional)
  • 5 Any special note (optional)

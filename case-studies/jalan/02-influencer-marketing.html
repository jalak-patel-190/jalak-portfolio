Case 02 · Influencer marketing

Influencer marketing on a monthly budget

Finding, negotiating with and directing influencers within a monthly budget, with an emphasis on clear booking records.

Context

A small budget, a long list of names

Influencer work at Jalan ran on a monthly budget. The work involved finding people who fit the product, agreeing terms, briefing them, and keeping booking decisions organised. The available archive does not verify totals or campaign outcomes.

Evidence boundary

Track the work without overstating the result

The available portfolio archive supports the scope of influencer coordination, but it does not contain a source export that verifies booking totals or spend. No booking count, spend total, or return-on-investment figure is published here.

Reach, enquiries, and sales per influencer also remain unverified, so this case focuses on the operating process rather than claiming campaign performance.

Results

What can be stated responsibly

The work involved sourcing, negotiating with, briefing, and coordinating influencers on a monthly budget. Without a verified booking log and campaign-level outcome data, a numerical result or ROI claim would overstate the available evidence.

What this showed

Coordination matters, but performance needs evidence

Influencer activity requires selection, negotiation, briefing, and follow-through. The next step for a stronger case is to connect verified bookings with reach, enquiries, and sales outcomes.

Case 06 · Swad

FROM A FEW THOUSAND TO 7.38M VIEWS

Chef Sneha Thakkar Recipes was handed over thin. Inside one year it reached about 50K subscribers and 7.38M+ views.

~5K→50K subs7.38M+ views1 year curve

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

The owner IP channel launched with a handful of videos and a few thousand subscribers.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

A personal brand channel without a growth system stays idle.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

Apply the same YouTube growth system used on the institute channel, starting with the first managed upload.

Execution

WHAT RAN

First managed upload reached about 30K views in a day. Channel compounded to about 50K subscribers and 7.38M+ views by exit.

Evidence

SIGNAL

Growth shape

Direction of the curve

Full curve sits inside a single year of management.

Takeaway

WHAT THIS SHOWED

Owner channels respond to systems the same way institute channels do.

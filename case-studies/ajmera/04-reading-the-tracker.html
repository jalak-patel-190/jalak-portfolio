Case 04 · Measurement

Reading the tracker carefully

How account scope and date windows change the meaning of a comparison across roughly fifty tracked accounts.

Context

Many accounts, easy to misread

Ajmera’s cross-platform tracker covered about fifty accounts. Without fixed windows and clear scope, growth claims could mix different start dates or channel sets.

Problem

Comparisons that are not like-for-like

Mixing partial periods or incomplete account lists makes percentage changes look stronger or weaker than the work supports.

Strategy

Same accounts, fixed dates

Lock the account set and the date range before comparing.

Takeaway

Measurement is a discipline

The tracker only helps when the rules of comparison stay stable enough for a reviewer to trust the number.

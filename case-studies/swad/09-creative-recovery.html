Case 09 · Swad

WHEN FOOTAGE FAILS, WRITE AROUND IT

Hardware loss made a reshoot impossible. Surviving clips became a comedy format, then a deliberate series, then a horror experiment.

Lost footage constraintComedy formatHorror experiment

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

Shoots fail. Budgets do not always allow a full reshoot.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

Scrapping partial footage wastes the day and the team.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

Write a comedy script around the partial clips. If it works, repeat once. Then change the genre so the trick stays fresh.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Comedy recovery format used twice. Third attempt shifted to horror with the strategist on camera. Lower views, strong qualitative response.

Evidence

SIGNAL

Mix of effort

Constraint as the creative brief.

Takeaway

WHAT THIS SHOWED

Recovery formats can become productised, but only while they still feel new.

Case 04 · Gujarati multi-format distribution

One film, multiple release formats

How Gujarati film catalogue assets were distributed through full releases, serialised parts, Shorts, and Facebook cuts, while keeping pre-tenure performance separate.

17.77M Rakhewal tenure views 163 YouTube pieces from one film 6.42M Radha Tara re-release views 6.7× posts in the compared Facebook periods

Context

A film can have more than one useful release format

For Gujarati titles, the work covered full releases, serialised parts, Shorts, and Facebook cuts. Each format offered a different way for people to discover or continue watching a title.

Problem

A single upload does not use every available format

Publishing a film once limits the number of entry points into the catalogue. The decision was to use different formats where appropriate, while clearly separating views from fresh tenure releases and repackaged content from older titles.

Strategy

Match the format to the job

Full releases keep the complete film available; serialised parts create more individual viewing entry points; Shorts extract moments for discovery; Facebook cuts provide another distribution surface. These are distinct formats, so their view totals are reported separately before being combined.

Facebook recovery: publishing volume after restriction

The page was inactive for about ten months between 18 May 2025 and 25 March 2026. The 2025 period recorded 65 posts and 26,325,860 views; the 2026 period recorded 433 posts and 47,351,230 views. The higher posting volume and view totals are observed together, but this comparison alone does not prove posting volume caused the change.

Results

One title, multiple tracked distribution outputs

Rakhewal generated 17,773,187 views across 163 YouTube pieces during the tenure distribution run. Radha Tara’s July 2026 serialised re-release reached 6,423,457 views across 15 parts, compared with 3,785,146 lifetime views on the 2023 original. Facebook publishing resumed after a long restriction period; its before-and-after totals are reported as observed outcomes, not proof of causation.

Takeaway

Distribution is a packaging decision as well as a publishing decision

The work was not just to post more. It was to choose how a title could be broken into formats, publish those formats, and keep the evidence clear about original releases, repackaged content, and inherited views.

Short-form and recovery · Swad Cooking Institute

A short-form engine, and creative recovery from lost footage

Shorts system tied to long videos, and recovery when source footage was lost so the schedule did not stop.

Context

Shorts had to support the long videos

Short-form was not a separate brand. It had to pull people toward the long recipe uploads and keep the channel active between shoots.

Execution

Three to four Shorts per long video

Each long recipe was broken into short clips for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts so the same shoot fed both formats.

Work output

Selected Instagram reels

What this showed

Constraints are a creative skill

Losing footage could have wasted a shoot. Turning the surviving clips into a story is the kind of problem solving a creative lead is paid for.

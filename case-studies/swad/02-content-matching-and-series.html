Cases 03 and 04 · Swad Cooking Institute

MATCHING CONTENT TO THE CHEF, AND THE MONDAY MISTAKE SERIES

A how-to-store series built from one familiar topic, and a weekly series built around mistakes everyone makes.

359K top how-to video views

Context

THE CHEFS ON CAMERA HAD DIFFERENT STRENGTHS

Each chef on camera had different strengths. Content had to match the chef, then the viewer, or performance stayed flat.

Strategy

MATCH THE TOPIC TO THE CHEF, THEN TO THE VIEWER

Topics were assigned by who could deliver them, not by a generic calendar. That improved both quality and audience fit.

Execution

THE HOW TO STORE SERIES

A series on how to store ingredients and leftovers, built from a familiar need. Top video reached about 359K views.

Series

A WEEKLY SERIES ON THE MISTAKES EVERYONE MAKES

Monday Mistake gave viewers a recurring reason to return. The format was simple and repeatable.

Result

MONDAY MISTAKE

A named weekly series that fitted the chef strengths and the audience habit of learning from common errors.

Takeaway

NOVELTY IS NOT WHAT VIEWERS WANT

Viewers wanted useful, familiar problems solved clearly. Matching chef to topic beat novelty for its own sake.

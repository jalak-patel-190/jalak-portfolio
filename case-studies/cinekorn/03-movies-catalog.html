Case 03 · Movies catalogue

Movies catalogue operations

Separating 65.48M lifetime channel views from the 3.50M views generated by 89 videos published during tenure.

65.48Mlifetime channel views
3.50Mtenure views · 89 videos

Context

Large catalogues hide tenure work

Channel lifetime numbers include years of prior publishing. Without a clean split, it is easy to over-claim or under-explain what happened during a specific tenure window.

Problem

One number is not a story

Reporting only lifetime views mixes inherited performance with work published while I was responsible for the channel.

Strategy

Separate inherited from tenure

Measure the channel total, then isolate views from videos published in the tenure window so both figures stay honest.

Evidence

Movies channel analytics

Takeaway

Attribution before ambition

Clear tenure math makes catalogue work defensible in reviews and portfolio claims.

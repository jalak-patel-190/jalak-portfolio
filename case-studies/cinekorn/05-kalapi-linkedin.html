Case 05 · Kalapi LinkedIn

Founder Presence, Not Just a Company Page

Narrative, cadence, and positioning for Kalapi Nagada on LinkedIn, run alongside the corporate channel so the founder voice stays distinct.

+265% average daily impressions +460% average daily engagements +0.48 pts engagement rate +93% followers per day

Context

A founder channel next to a company channel

Kalapi Nagada is a founder of Cinekorn Entertainment. The personal LinkedIn presence needed to reflect industry positioning without becoming a second company bulletin.

Problem

Company updates are not founder narrative

If the founder feed only repeats announcements, it adds little beyond the corporate page. The gap was a distinct point of view: how the business of entertainment is read from the inside.

Strategy

Separate roles, shared standards

Company page = institutional voice. Founder profile = perspective, pattern recognition, and industry context. Cadence and topic selection were managed so the two feeds reinforced rather than duplicated each other.

Evidence

Profile and performance surface

Founder feed priorities

Editorial themes, not measured percentages

Industry context
Business perspective
Selective company updates

Operating loop

How the founder page was run

Read signals from the entertainment industry
Draft and frame the founder's point of view
Publish on a planned cadence
Review audience response

Results

A clearer founder lane

The supplied comparison covered 196 days before the operating period and 169 days after it began on 31 March 2026. Total impressions rose from 19,581 to 61,570 and engagements from 176 to 850. Because the windows differ, average daily rates are the fair comparison: impressions rose from about 100 to 364 per day (+265%), and engagements from about 0.90 to 5.03 per day (+460%). Engagement rate moved from 0.90% to 1.38%, a gain of 0.48 percentage points. Followers per day rose from 3.15 to 6.09 (+93%). Thirty-seven of the top 50 posts in the supplied ranking were published after 31 March 2026.

The founder feed carried industry and business framing instead of only company announcements. It sat alongside the corporate page without replacing it.

Takeaway

What this showed

Founder LinkedIn works best when it is treated as a distinct narrative system, not as a mirror of the company page.

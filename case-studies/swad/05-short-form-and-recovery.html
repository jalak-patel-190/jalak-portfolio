Case 05 · Swad Cooking Institute

A short-form engine, and creative recovery from lost footage

Shorts system tied to long videos, and recovery when source footage was lost so the schedule did not stop.

Context

Shorts had to support the long videos

Short-form was not a separate brand. It had to pull people toward the long recipe uploads and keep the channel active between shoots.

Execution

Three to four Shorts per long video

Each long video was planned to yield multiple Shorts so the same shoot fed both formats.

Role on each reel varied: some were fully shot and edited by me, some were directed on set and edited, some were concept-only when I could not be on the shoot that day.

When footage was lost

When source footage was lost, the work was to recover a publishable cut from what remained and keep the schedule moving.

Work output

Instagram reels from the short-form system

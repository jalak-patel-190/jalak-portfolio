Workshop operations · Swad Cooking Institute

Workshop operations, and the watch hours versus revenue gap

I ran the workshop back end each month, and diagnosed why daily livestreams were not turning into revenue.

Context

The workshops needed a back end

Each new month began with the workshop schedule. I managed the live workshop calendar and the operations behind every free and paid YouTube workshop.

Diagnosis

Watch hours were high, but revenue was not following

Watch hours were strong, but estimated revenue did not scale with them. Daily livestreams drew strong views on the day, then went largely dead as recordings.

Recommendation

Fewer lives, edited uploads

Reduce raw livestream output and upload tightly edited recipe videos. Edited uploads attract new viewers and repeat views.

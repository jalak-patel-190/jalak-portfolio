Case 01 · Jalan

A CHANNEL FOR EACH SEGMENT

Ten to fifteen YouTube channels for a new B2B textile brand, each aimed at a product or regional slice instead of one generic feed.

10-15 channelsRegional splitsB2B audience

Context

NO EXISTING YOUTUBE PRESENCE

Jalan launched without a YouTube system. The brief was to create presence that could serve wholesale buyers and regional product interest, not a single brand showreel.

Problem

ONE CHANNEL CANNOT SERVE EVERY SEGMENT

Product categories and language markets behave differently. A single main channel would bury regional demand under generic product posts.

Strategy

PORTFOLIO, NOT A MONOLITH

Build a set of channels tied to product category or regional audience, with a main brand channel as the hub.

Execution

WHAT WENT LIVE

Main Jalan channel plus Marathi and Gujarati Shorts channels, with additional product-specific and regional properties making up the rest of the 10 to 15 channel set.

Case 02 · Swad Cooking Institute

Matching content to the chef, and the Monday Mistake series

A how-to-store series built from one familiar topic, and a weekly series built around mistakes everyone makes.

359K top how-to views Series logic per upload

Context

Chefs on camera had different strengths

Each chef had different strengths on camera. Content had to match the chef first, then the viewer, or performance stayed flat.

Strategy

Match the topic to the chef, then to the viewer

Topics were assigned by who could deliver them, not by a generic calendar. That improved quality and audience fit.

Execution

The How to Store series

A series on how to store ingredients and leftovers, built from a familiar kitchen need. The top video reached about 359K views.

Monday Mistake

A named weekly series on mistakes everyone makes. Simple, repeatable format that gave viewers a reason to return every week.

Takeaway

Novelty is not what viewers want

Viewers wanted useful, familiar problems solved clearly. Matching chef to topic beat novelty for its own sake.

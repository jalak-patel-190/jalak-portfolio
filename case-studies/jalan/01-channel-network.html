Case 01 · Jalan Mega Mart

Building the channel and page network

Building and maintaining a channel and page network to support retail demand and local-language reach.

Multi-channel presence Local language reach Retail demand support

Context

A network of channels and pages

Jalan Mega Mart needed a workable social and YouTube network. The store sold textiles across regions. One page and one language could not carry product demand, festival offers, or local search interest.

The job was to set up and operate channels and pages that could carry product and festival demand without a large media team.

Problem

One surface was not enough

A single company page left gaps in language, product category, and festival timing. Buyers searching in regional terms did not land on a consistent public presence.

Execution

Channels, pages, and cadence

Map product and language needs
Set up channels and pages
Name and structure each property
Run posting cadence across the network

Channel and page setup, naming, and posting cadence across the network so the store had a consistent public presence for product and festival demand.

Language channels

Regional YouTube network

Separate language channels carried product and festival demand in the languages buyers actually searched and watched.

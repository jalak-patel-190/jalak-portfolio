Case 01 · Jalan Mega Mart

BUILDING THE CHANNEL AND PAGE NETWORK

When I joined, the company had almost no presence. Within about a year I had built a network of 10 to 15 YouTube channels and 5 or more Instagram and Facebook pages.

10 to 15 YouTube channels5+ Instagram and Facebook pages1 person running it

Context

A COMPANY WITH NO AUDIENCE TO SPEAK TO

Jalan Mega Mart was new, and nobody was talking to its buyers. The business needed to be visible to retailers across regions, in several languages, before a sales team could work from it.

Problem

ONE CHANNEL WOULD NOT COVER THE MARKET

A single channel cannot speak to a Gujarati buyer, a Marathi buyer and a product buyer at the same time. The network had to be split by audience and by product, and each part had to be run without a team of specialists.

Strategy

SPLIT BY AUDIENCE AND PRODUCT

Main channel
Language channels (Marathi, Gujarati)
Shorts channels
Instagram and Facebook pages

Execution

WHAT I BUILT AND RAN

  • Created 10 to 15 new YouTube channels, each with a clear audience or product purpose.
  • Set up and ran 5 or more Instagram and Facebook pages.
  • Handled all social platforms on my own, from planning to posting.
  • Built the monthly content calendar and the festival offer campaigns.

Results

WHAT I CAN SHOW NOW

What this showed

A NETWORK IS A SYSTEM, NOT A POST

Building a network means deciding who each channel is for before you post. That decision, made early, is what made one person able to run the whole set.

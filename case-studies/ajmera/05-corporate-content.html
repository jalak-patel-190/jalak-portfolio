FROM OFFICE ACTIVITIES TO COMPANY CONTENT

Turning employee activities, festivals and office moments into corporate social content with HR support and creative production coordination.

8 public examples across the corporate content track

Context

THE OFFICE HAD CONTENT. IT WAS NOT PUBLISHED AS A SYSTEM.

Festivals, employee moments and office life were already happening. The work was to turn them into a consistent corporate content track with HR support and creative production coordination.

Problem

MOMENTS STAYED INTERNAL

Without a capture and publish process, employee and festival moments remained internal. The company page and social channels underused the people already in the building.

Strategy

FROM OFFICE ACTIVITY TO COMPANY CONTENT

Plan capture around real calendar events. Coordinate HR and production so festivals, employee activities and office moments become publishable corporate stories instead of one-off phone clips.

Execution

PRODUCTION COORDINATION AND BTS

Eight public examples sit on the corporate content track. Behind-the-scenes production was documented so the process is visible, not only the finished posts.

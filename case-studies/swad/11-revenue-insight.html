Case 11 · Swad

WATCH HOURS ARE NOT REVENUE

Daily lives drew strong same-day engagement but died as assets. The recommendation was edited recipe uploads plus membership as a lever.

Lives same-dayEdited VOD durableMembership lever

Context

WHERE THIS SAT

Ad revenue was not scaling with watch hours despite steady livestream output.

Problem

WHAT BLOCKED GROWTH

Fifty-minute live recordings full of off-topic chat do not get rewatched, so they stop earning.

Strategy

THE CHOICE

Introduce YouTube Membership as one lever. Reduce raw livestream volume in favour of tightly edited recipe videos that attract new and repeat views.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Diagnosis delivered to the company. Membership introduced. Full adoption of the edit-led slate was incomplete.

Evidence

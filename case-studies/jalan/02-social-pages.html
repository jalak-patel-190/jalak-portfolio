FIVE-PLUS PAGES, ONE CALENDAR

Instagram and Facebook presence across product and regional markets, run with a daily posting rhythm from a thin team.

5+ pagesIG + FB platformsDaily cadence

Context

SOCIAL STARTED AT ZERO

There were no existing Instagram or Facebook pages to optimise. Presence had to be created by category and region.

Problem

WITHOUT A CALENDAR, PAGES DIE

Multiple pages without a shared content rhythm produce uneven quality and gaps that kill algorithmic reach.

Strategy

CALENDAR FIRST

One content calendar governing daily posting across product and regional pages, managed hands-on while the team was still thin.

Execution

WHAT RAN

Five or more Instagram and Facebook pages spanning product categories and regional markets, with ongoing posting and creative direction.

Takeaway

CADENCE BEATS SPORADIC HERO POSTS

Case 04 · Jalan Mega Mart

Festival demand and content planning

Aligning product push, channel content, and retailer timing around festival demand peaks — an operations rhythm, not a decorative calendar.

Context

Demand clusters around festivals

Festival periods concentrate buyer attention and stock decisions. Content and outreach had to match those windows.

Strategy

Plan the season, not just the day

Map major festival windows, decide which products and formats lead each phase, and sequence channel work so retailers and callers are not surprised by spikes.

What this showed

Calendar as operations, not decoration

Festival planning worked when treated as demand logistics: what to push, when to publish, and who follows up.

Case 06 · Company LinkedIn

Company Content That Explains the Industry

The company page became more consistent and more editorial. Performance rose with zero paid spend.

0% impressions
0% clicks
0% reactions

Context

A page that needed a reason to publish

I have managed the company page since April 13, 2026. Before that period, the page depended mainly on company announcements and posted less often.

Problem

Announcement-only content is thin

A distribution company sits inside the entertainment business. Publishing only about itself leaves out the industry context that makes the brand useful to follow.

Strategy

Editorial presence, not only updates

Content was designed around information that could make someone stop and understand an industry development, not only maximise reactions.

Evidence

Before and after

Company LinkedIn: before vs after

Zero paid spend across the comparison window

  • Before: 6,263 imp · 1,230 clicks · 94 reactions
  • After: 17,289 imp · 2,838 clicks · 525 reactions

Impressions +278%. Clicks +216%. Reactions +665%. Publishing moved from about 1 post / 8.1 days to 1 / 2.4 days.

MetricBeforeAfter

Publishing frequency: about 1 post every 8.1 days before, 1 post every 2.4 days after (about 3.4×). Zero paid spend.

Results

More consistent, more editorial

The company page had a broader reason to publish: Cinekorn could use its position inside the entertainment business to explain what was happening around it.

Takeaway

What this showed

Company content does not always need to talk about the company. Sometimes the strongest way to establish presence is to demonstrate what the company understands.

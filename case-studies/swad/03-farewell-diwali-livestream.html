Case 05 · Swad Cooking Institute

THE FAREWELL DIWALI LIVESTREAM

When I resigned, I wanted to thank the team and the audience properly. The result was one continuous five hour thank you, with 20 recipes from six chefs.

5h 34m one continuous livestream20 recipes6 chefs

Context

A THANK YOU, NOT A GROWTH PLAY

I knew I would miss the team and the audience we had built. This livestream was a tribute, not a growth or revenue tactic.

Execution

ONE LIVESTREAM, TWENTY RECIPES

Plan the Diwali menu
Assign recipes across six chefs
Run one continuous session
Teach all 20 recipes live

Evidence

Results

WHAT IT WAS FOR

No views or revenue target was set for this stream. It was a farewell to the audience, and it was run as one continuous session so the audience could stay with the team from start to finish.

The team

A PRE-DIWALI CELEBRATION

What this showed

CLOSE WELL, NOT JUST PERFORM WELL

How a piece of work ends matters as much as how it runs. This was the way I wanted to leave the audience that had grown with the channel.

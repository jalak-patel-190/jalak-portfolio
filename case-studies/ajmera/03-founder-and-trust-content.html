Case 03 · Founder and trust

Founder and trust content

Building founder-led and trust-oriented content so buyers could see the people and process behind the product.

Context

B2B buyers care who they deal with

Product shots alone did not answer credibility questions. Founder presence and process stories helped reduce uncertainty for regional buyers.

Problem

Catalogue without character

When every post looked like inventory, the brand felt interchangeable. Trust had to be visible in the feed, not only on the shop floor.

Strategy

Put people next to product

Balance product publishing with founder-facing and process content so the account signals reliability as well as range.

Takeaway

Trust is a content job

In regional B2B textile, showing who stands behind the brand is part of the distribution system, not a soft extra.

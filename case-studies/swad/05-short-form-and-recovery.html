Case 09 · Context

FOOTAGE WAS LOST, AND THE REST HAD TO BE SAVED

Some footage was lost to hardware failure, and a reshoot was not possible. Rather than drop the surviving clips, I built a comedic script around them so the content was not wasted.

Case 09 · Execution

THE RECOVERY REELS

Salvage the recipe clips
Write a comic script around them
Shoot the script
Merge the recipe into the story
Repeat the format where it works

Case 09 · Evidence

THREE REELS FROM ONE LOSS

Recovery reel one Recovery reel two Horror concept reel, I acted in it

The third time the same trick would not have felt fresh, so I pitched a horror concept. It drew strong praise for the creative risk, even though its numbers were below the first two.

As creative lead on this stream, every reel passed through my review, and some were built fully from my own ideas.

Between uploads

COMMUNITY POSTS

When a video was not live or a new upload was not out yet, I used YouTube community posts to keep the audience engaged. The aim was to bring people back, invite replies and encourage shares.
